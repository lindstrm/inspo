# Surface: The Light Table (main gallery)

**Scope:** the app's single primary surface — gallery wall, capture, and projection (detail) view.
**Mode:** Operate.

**Audience & job:** the owner, mid-project, doing one of three things: saving something inspiring (capture), browsing for direction (recall), or copying vocabulary out (hand-off).

**Task & actions:** paste URL / drop image anywhere (fire-and-forget; analysis develops in background); scan the wall of everything; filter by design-type tray; open a slide to projection; copy keywords, image prompt, or build brief in one click.

**Content & proof:** the owner's real saves only. Empty states must be honest — no fabricated inventory; the empty light table invites the first save.

**Chosen direction:** The Slide Library (see DESIGN.md). Memorable moment: a fresh save landing as an amber "developing" slide that resolves into a typed, labeled mount — taste becoming vocabulary in front of you.

**Constraints:** density over curation; instant mechanical state changes; images outglow chrome always; keyboard-reachable capture and copy actions.

**Unresolved:** exact module dimensions and grid gaps; projection-view transition implementation (View Transitions vs. manual); tray overflow behavior at many design types.
